How to play

  • Move — drag anywhere on the left side of the screen.
  • Throw — hold on the right side, drag to aim, let go. Hold longer to throw farther; the target ring shows where it'll turn around. Letting go of the drag keeps your aim.
  • Catch it — it bounces off walls on the way out and flies back to you, but if a wall is in the way it gets stuck there. Walk over it, or hold the right side to call it back — it's pulled straight at you like gravity and slides along walls (but can get stuck in a corner). It hits anyone in its way!
  • Two hands — you can carry two boomerangs. When a snack is knocked out their boomerangs turn grey: anyone can grab them.
  • Slash — the red button. Deadly with a boomerang in hand; without one it's a shove (push people into hot sauce — that counts as your knockout). It also swats incoming boomerangs to the floor.
  • Dash — the blue button. Dodge, or hop over hot-sauce pits.
  • One hit and you're out for the round. Every knockout is a point (knocking yourself out costs one). First to the target wins.
  • Mystery power-ups (glowing ?) appear during each round — you only find out what you got when you grab it. They stay with you between rounds; hold up to 3 (a 4th pushes out your oldest): Explosive (used up — a new one appears) Extra boomerang Fire trail Ice trail (freezes) Triple Telekinesis (hold to steer) Teleport (dash to your boomerang) Drill (through walls) Speedy feet Shield Disguise (stand still) Decoy (dash) Bamboozled — avoid! flips your controls
  • Curve — right after throwing, drag on the right side (or push the right stick) to bend your boomerang around corners.
  • Arenas — hide in tall grass (others can't see you unless they're close), slide on ice, and step on purple pads to teleport across the map.
  • Up to 8 fighters — big arenas show up automatically for 7–8 players. Teams: red vs blue, no friendly fire.
  • Keyboard: WASD move, J throw/call, L slash, K dash. Player 2: arrows, / throw, , slash, . dash.
  • Online: one person taps Host a game and shares the 4-letter code; friends tap Join. The server runs the match, so everyone sees the same thing.
  • Controllers (Bluetooth works on iPhone/iPad): left stick move, right stick aim, A/RT throw (hold to call back), X/RB slash, B/LT dash.
